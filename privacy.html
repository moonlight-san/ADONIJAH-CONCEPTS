2. What we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, phone number, email address and the details of your project, when you email us, message us on WhatsApp or call us.
  • Technical data: this website loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads. We do not run analytics or advertising trackers on this site at the time of writing.

The contact form does not store your details on our website. When you press Send Message, it opens your email app with your message ready to send. When you use the WhatsApp button, it opens WhatsApp. Nothing reaches us until you press send there.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss your project.
  • To prepare consultations and quotations and, if you hire us, to carry out the work.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Our legal basis

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, we rely on your consent (when you contact us), steps taken at your request before a contract, performance of a contract, our legitimate interest in running a construction business, and legal obligations.

5. Who we share it with

  • Service providers that carry our communications and run the website, such as WhatsApp (Meta), Google (Gmail) and our hosting provider.
  • Professionals involved in your project, such as architects, engineers, surveyors and suppliers, only as needed and with your knowledge.
  • Regulators, courts or other authorities where the law requires it.

WhatsApp and Gmail have their own privacy policies.

6. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, including any legal, tax or accounting requirement. After that we delete or anonymise it.
